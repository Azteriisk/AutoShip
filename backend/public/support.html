Frequently Asked Questions

Do merchants need their own EasyPost or carrier accounts?

No! AutoShip provides pre-negotiated commercial base postage through USPS, UPS, and FedEx right out of the box as part of the service.

What Clover devices are supported?

AutoShip supports all Clover POS hardware, including Clover Station Solo/Duo, Clover Mini, and handheld Clover Flex terminals.

How do I print shipping labels?

Labels can be printed to any 4"×6" thermal label printer (Zebra, Rollo, Dymo) directly from the AutoShip Merchant Web Dashboard or downloaded as vector PDF/SVG.
