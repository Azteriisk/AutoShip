AutoShip for Clover® POS

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

AutoShip ("we", "our", or "us") provides a shipping rate calculation, packaging optimization, and postage fulfillment application designed for Clover POS merchants and their customers. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data processed through our application.

1. Information We Collect

To calculate shipping rates and purchase carrier postage, AutoShip processes the following data solely on behalf of the merchant:

  • Shipping Destination Data: Recipient name, delivery address, city, state, postal code, and country.
  • Order Details: Item names, item dimensions, and item weights necessary for box calculation.
  • Postage & Tracking: Shipping labels generated, tracking numbers, and selected carrier service levels.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To generate real-time shipping rate quotes from supported carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx).
  • To calculate optimal packaging geometry using 3D bin packing algorithms.
  • To purchase postage labels and provide tracking updates to merchants and their customers.
  • To synchronize subscription usage with the Clover App Market billing APIs.

3. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We share destination addresses, package weights, and dimensions exclusively with integrated carrier APIs (EasyPost, USPS, UPS, FedEx) for the sole purpose of quoting and generating postage labels.

We never sell, rent, or monetize merchant or customer personal data.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between the Clover POS terminal, AutoShip backend services, and carrier networks is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. Carrier credentials and OAuth access tokens are stored securely using encrypted key management.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy compliance team at privacy@autoship.azterisk.net.
